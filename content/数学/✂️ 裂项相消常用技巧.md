# ✂️ 裂项相消常用技巧

- $\frac{1}{n(n+1)} = \frac{1}{n} - \frac{1}{n+1}$
- $\frac{1}{n(n+k)} = \frac{1}{k}\left( \frac{1}{n} - \frac{1}{n+k} \right)$（$k$为常数，$k \in \mathbb{N}^*$）
- $\frac{2}{n(n+1)(n+2)} = \frac{1}{n(n+1)} - \frac{1}{(n+1)(n+2)}$
- $\frac{1}{\sqrt{n} + \sqrt{n+k}} = \frac{1}{k}(\sqrt{n+k} - \sqrt{n})$
